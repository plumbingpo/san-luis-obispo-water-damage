Where Water Damage Starts in Los Angeles Properties

Every house has a few weak points where water tends to get loose. These are the ones Los Angeles property owners report to us again and again.

Water heaters: Sediment collects in the bottom of a tank and slowly eats through the steel. The first sign is often a rusty puddle or a damp ring on the floor around the base. If the tank ruptures, it keeps refilling and draining onto the floor until someone closes the cold supply valve. Heaters in a hallway closet or attic can ruin flooring and ceilings well beyond the utility area.

Roofs: One strong storm is enough to peel back shingles and push water beneath the roof covering. Skylights, plumbing vents and chimneys are other entry points once their seals crack with age. Since water follows the framing downhill, the ceiling stain rarely sits directly below the actual opening. For roof leaks in Los Angeles, we tarp where needed, pull out soaked insulation and dry the structure so the ceiling can be repaired.

Freezing: Cold nights, depending on the season, can break plumbing that showed no sign of trouble earlier. The first to go are hose bibs, irrigation lines and any pipe routed through an unheated space. Vacant homes and rooms with the heat turned down are especially exposed. If a faucet only trickles on a freezing morning, shut off the water and call before the pipe thaws.

Backups: Gurgling sounds and more than one slow fixture at once suggest the main drain is partly blocked. When the clog becomes complete, every flush and every load of laundry comes back out downstairs. The water is unsanitary, and hard surfaces need disinfecting after the solids and liquid are removed. Stay out of the affected rooms and avoid running water until the line is cleared.

Seepage: A wet line along the bottom of a foundation wall is a sign that water is building up in the soil next to it. Short downspouts, soil that slopes toward the house and blocked footing drains are frequent causes. Early clues include powdery white residue on the concrete, flaking wall paint and air that always smells musty. The team at San Luis Obispo Restoration can extract standing water and dry the space, and we will tell you what we think let it in.
